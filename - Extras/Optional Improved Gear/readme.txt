This mod simply improves nearly all of the gear in MUA1.

This optional mod is compatible with EXG but make sure this one overwrites the EXG version.

All Changes:
Balanced out the sell amount of all gear based upon how valuable or powerful each gear is
Joybuzzer now does 15% of melee attacks will paralyze victim instead of 5%
Attuma's Wrath power dmg scaled from 25% to 30%
Blackheart's Corruption dmg dealt from 5% to 15% + removed the xp taken away affect
Bulldozer's Helmet now reflects 10% of dmg instead of 5%
Assassin's Sight 15% critical chance on ranged attacks instead of 5%
Crown of Byrrah 3 to Body, Focus and Strike instead of 2
Super Soldier Serum 25% max health instead of 10%
Dark Steel increased defense from 25% to 30%
Spider's Bite increased chance to dodge melee attacks from 10% to 15% + increased movement and attack speed from 20% to 25%
Dark Uru Hammer increased dmg to all melee attacks from 10 to 15
Deathbird's Feather increased HP regen from 5 to 10
Doom's Mask increased all talents from 1 to 3 + increased max health and energy from 10% to 25%
Dragon Polymer increased defense from 25% to 30%
Dragon's Heart increased critical chance on melee attacks from 3% to 10%
Armor of Galactus increased absorb dmg from 10% to 15%
Imperial Mantle increased Body, Focus and Strike from 6 to 7
Stone Touch increased chance to turn victim into stone from 3% to 7%
Lizard's Serum increased HP regen from 2 to 10
Loki's Gift now does increased attack dmg instead of reducing by 25%
Norn Stones increased XP from 15% to 25%
White Ring of Mandarin increased energy dmg to all melee attacks from 5 to 10 + energy dmg decreased from 35% to 30%
MODOK Chip increased XP from 5% to 15%
Master Hologram increased chance to dodge melee attacks from 10% to 25%
Grappling Speed increased grab move dmg from 50% to 65%
Radioactive Touch increased melee dmg as radiation dmg from 5% to 10%
Rhino's Horn increased resist all from 10% to 15%
Scorpion's Tail increased chance to cause knockback on ranged attacks from 10% to 25%
Shocker's Gloves increased electricity dmg to all melee attacks from 10 to 15
Starbolt's Flame increased fire dmg to all melee attacks from 5 to 10 + increased all fire dmg powers from 35% to 50%
Hydro Suit increased energy and HP per knockout from 10 to 15
Helmet of Titannus increased strike from 10 to 15
Ulik's Fists increased critical chance on all attacks from 10% to 15%
Ultimo's Plating increased chance to deflect projectiles from 10% to 25%
Shi'ar Battle Armor increased reflect dmg from 10% to 15%
Bionic Arm increased strike from 5 to 10
Wrecker's Crowbar increased chance to deflect projectiles from 15% to 25% + increased melee dmg from 5% to 30%
Due to the large amount of gear changes and edits, its quite possible I missed some changes that aren't documented here
